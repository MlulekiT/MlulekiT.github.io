Senior Lecturer, Department of Population Studies and Demography, North-West University (Mahikeng). My research examines how demographic change, household relationships, kinship structures and social inequalities shape health across the life course in African populations.

Four research pillars

Maternal, Reproductive & Early-Life Health

Contraception, fertility, maternal healthcare, child health and survival.

Health Inequalities

Households & Population Dynamics

Family formation, kinship, living arrangements and demographic change.

Ageing & Healthy Longevity

Multimorbidity, disability, functional ageing and care dependence.

Across the life course

Early Life

Child mortality, nutrition and immunisation.

Adolescence

Sexual debut, reproductive health and transitions to adulthood.

Reproductive Adulthood

Contraception, fertility, maternal health and intimate partner violence.

Family Formation

Households, kinship, caregiving and intergenerational support.

Later Adulthood

Chronic disease, multimorbidity and healthcare access.

Older Age

Disability, functional limitation and healthy ageing.

Current Work

Relationship Commitment and Contraceptive Use

Examining how relationship context influences contraceptive use among AGYW, using SADHS 2016.

Sex Differentials in Sexual Debut

Survival analysis of the timing of sexual debut among AGYW, using pooled DHS data and a life-course framework.

Pooled SADC Analyses

Extending DHS Methodological Report No. 36 across multiple SADC countries — shared-frailty survival models.

Population Health Tools

Developing open-source Stata mapping tools, including mtmaps, and modernising the PHRE web presence.
