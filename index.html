Dr Mluleki Tsawe

Population Health Across the Life Course in Sub-Saharan Africa

Senior Lecturer, Department of Population Studies and Demography, North-West University (Mahikeng).

My research examines how demographic change, household relationships, kinship structures and social inequalities shape health across the life course in African populations.

Research Areas

Sexual & Reproductive Health

Contraceptive use, fertility intentions, sexual debut and reproductive transitions.

Maternal & Child Health

Maternal healthcare utilisation, child survival, immunisation and health inequalities.

Households & Kinship

Family formation, caregiving, living arrangements and intergenerational support.

Population Ageing

Healthy ageing, multimorbidity, disability and care dependence.
