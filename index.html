Research identity

Population health through a demographic and life-course lens

My research sits at the intersection of population studies, demography and population health. I am particularly interested in how demographic processes, household structures and inequalities influence health at different stages of life.

Central research question

How do demographic change, household relationships and social inequalities shape health across the life course in African populations?

Four research pillars
01

Maternal, Reproductive & Early-Life Health

Fertility, contraception, maternal healthcare, sexual and reproductive health, child health, survival and early-life inequalities.

02

Health Inequalities & Utilisation

Social determinants of health, healthcare utilisation, population-health disparities and unequal access to health-promoting resources.

03

Households & Population Dynamics

Family formation, kinship, living arrangements, intergenerational relationships, caregiving and demographic change.

04

Ageing & Healthy Longevity

Multimorbidity, disability, functional limitation, care dependence, social protection and the conditions associated with healthy ageing.

Life-course perspective
01

Survival, nutrition, immunisation and childhood health.

05

06

Older Age

Disability, functional limitation and healthy ageing.

Research programme

Current work

Current projects develop a coherent programme around population health, reproductive transitions, household relationships and ageing in African populations.

Reproductive health

Relationship Context and Contraceptive Use

Examining how relationship commitment and relationship context influence contraceptive use among adolescent girls and young women using South African survey data.

Demographic transitions

Timing of Sexual Debut

Survival analysis of sex differentials in the timing of sexual debut across Southern African populations, using pooled DHS data and a life-course framework.

Population health

Pooled SADC Analyses

Comparative analyses of demographic and health outcomes across Southern African countries, with attention to heterogeneity between populations and survey settings.

Ageing

Households, Kinship and Healthy Ageing

Developing an integrated research programme examining older-person household structure, kinship, multimorbidity, disability, care dependence and social protection.

Research methods

Quantitative population-health research

My methodological work focuses on extracting robust demographic and population-health evidence from large-scale African population datasets, while paying close attention to measurement, survey design and uncertainty.
