Research identity

Population health through a demographic and life-course lens

My research sits at the intersection of population studies, demography and public health, with a particular focus on how demographic structures, household contexts and social inequalities shape health and wellbeing across the life course in Sub-Saharan Africa.

Core question

How do population processes, social contexts, and inequalities shape health and wellbeing across the life course in Sub-Saharan Africa?

01

Maternal, reproductive & early-life health

Maternal healthcare utilisation, sexual and reproductive health, fertility-related behaviour, newborn care, and child health.

02

Health inequalities & utilisation

Social and demographic inequalities in health, healthcare access, service utilisation, disability, chronic conditionsy, and functioning.

03

Households & population dynamics

Household structure, kinship, family formation, intergenerational relationships, and changing population contexts.

04

Ageing & healthy longevity

Population ageing, multimorbidity, disability, care dependence, social protection, and the social conditions of healthy ageing.

Current research

Building a coherent research programme

Current work develops connected empirical strands around reproductive health, demographic behaviour, households, kinship and healthy ageing. The emphasis is on rigorous analysis of African population data and producing evidence that can inform scholarship, policy and practice.

Sexual & reproductive health

Relationship context and contraceptive use

Examining how partnership context, relationship characteristics, and household circumstances shape contraceptive behaviour among adolescent girls and young women in South Africa.

Demographic methods

Timing of sexual debut

Investigating the timing and determinants of sexual initiation using survival analysis and harmonised Demographic and Health Survey data across Southern Africa.

Comparative population health

Pooled SADC analyses

Comparative analyses across Southern African countries to identify demographic, socioeconomic and contextual inequalities in population, and reproductive health outcomes.

Ageing & households

Households, kinship and healthy ageing

Developing a research programme on older-person household structure, kinship, multimorbidity, disability, care dependence, and social protection in South Africa.

Academic profile

Demographer, population-health researcher and educator

I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Population Studies and Demography at North-West University, Mahikeng Campus.

My academic work focuses on the relationship between population dynamics, demographic behaviour, household contexts and population health in Sub-Saharan Africa. I work primarily with secondary population and health datasets, using quantitative demographic and statistical methods to generate evidence on health inequalities, service utilisation, reproductive health, households and ageing.

I also contribute to postgraduate research supervision, examination, research coordination and academic programme development. I lead the Population Dynamics sub-programme within the Population and Health Research Entity at North-West University.
